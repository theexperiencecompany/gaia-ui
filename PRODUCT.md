# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Frontend developers and indie hackers building AI assistants, chatbots, and conversational interfaces. They're evaluating component libraries while actively building — they want to ship fast and want components that feel right in production, not just in a demo. They arrive from GitHub, Discord, or referral. They decide in under a minute: does this look like something a careful team uses, or is it marketing fluff?

## Product Purpose

GAIA UI is a public component registry for conversational and AI assistant interfaces. The components are extracted from the GAIA AI assistant itself — battle-tested in production, not invented for the library. The site exists to showcase them and lower the friction of adoption. Success looks like a developer landing, trusting the taste, and running the install command.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, opinionated. The voice is direct and unboastful — "here's what we use, take it if it's useful." No hype, no superlatives. Confidence through specificity, not volume.

## Anti-references

- **MUI / Chakra UI docs:** Heavy, corporate, utility-first documentation aesthetic. Dense information hierarchy, municipal color palettes, components that feel like they were designed by committee.
- **SaaS component library template sites:** Gradient hero blobs, identical feature-benefit cards, gradient-clipped text headings, hero-metric blocks (big number + small label + supporting stats). Design for screenshots, not use.

## Design Principles

1. **Not for screenshots.** Every decision serves actual use — spacing, motion, color should reduce friction, not perform sophistication.
2. **Confidence without volume.** The copy and design say things once, clearly. No restatements, no filler, no superlatives.
3. **Details carry the message.** Spacing rhythm, type scale, and motion timing are how the library proves its own quality. The site is the component.
4. **Earned trust over stated trust.** Show the gallery, show the code, show the components working. Don't write about how good they are.
5. **Familiar until it surprises.** The structure should feel instantly readable (like Notion, Linear) — then one small, precise choice makes it feel distinctly GAIA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA sensible defaults. No hard requirements beyond that. Consider reduced motion where animation is present.
